Guesstimate Challenge

How many ping-pong balls can fit in a Boeing 747?

Test your consulting acumen. It's not about knowing the exact number, it's about your approach to structuring a complex, ambiguous problem.

01

Calculate Volume of 747

Assume passenger cabin is a cylinder: V = πr²h. Estimate length and radius.

02

Calculate Volume of Ball

Standard ball is ~40mm diameter. Calculate spherical volume.

03

Account for Packing Fraction

Spheres don't pack perfectly. Multiply total available volume by ~70-74%.
